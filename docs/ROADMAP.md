# Roadmap

## v2.0 — platform architecture

Pluggable telemetry, short-horizon world-model planning, multi-critic safety, uncertainty/OOD gating, intents/Pareto, multi-cell coordination, execution modes and rollback, hash-chain audit, offline Fitted-Q baseline, xApp/rApp orchestration, srsRAN JSON/E2 bridge boundaries, and resilience scenarios.

## v2.1 — calibrated lab integration

Recorded srsRAN trace package, bridge capability discovery, richer scheduler/KPM mapping, learned world-model calibration, experiment manifests/statistical report generation, and topology import.

## v2.2 — RIC-native packaging

O-RAN SC xApp packaging example, FlexRIC adapter example, A1 policy schemas, model artifact distribution workflow, and authenticated bridge transport.

## v3 research targets

Learned uncertainty calibration, graph-based multi-cell world model, constrained multi-agent RL baselines, safe exploration in digital twin, human-in-the-loop approval experiments, energy measurement calibration, and long-duration drift/rollback studies.
